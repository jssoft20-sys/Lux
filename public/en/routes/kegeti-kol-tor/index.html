Kegeti gorge and Lake Kol-Tor

60 min flight 30 min on the ground Landing at 2 700 m Airbus H125

What to expect

A landing beside the high-altitude lake Kol-Tor.

The route runs over the Kegeti gorge — mountain ridges, rivers and alpine meadows. Landing on the shore of Kol-Tor: thirty minutes by turquoise water surrounded by mountains.

  • The turquoise Lake Kol-Tor
  • Alpine meadows of the Kegeti gorge
  • Thirty minutes on the shore
  • Panorama of the Kyrgyz range
