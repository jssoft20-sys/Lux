Frequently asked questions

The answers people usually have to ask for in chat.

Message us on WhatsApp
How does booking work?
First we agree on the route. Then we choose a date — it depends on the aircraft schedule and the weather. After that you make a prepayment and we hold the slot for you.
What if the weather is not flyable?
The decision is made by the flight operations service and air traffic control, not by a manager. If they say the weather is not flyable, the flight is rescheduled to a date convenient for you. If no date works, we refund the prepayment in full.
What else can postpone a flight?
There are three reasons in total, and all of them are about safety, not our convenience.
1. Weather — decided by flight operations and air traffic control.
2. Technical — the aircraft is inspected before and after every flight. If an issue is found or unscheduled maintenance is needed, the flight is rescheduled.
3. State flights — the airspace is closed for special flights, emergencies and delegation visits.
In all three cases we act the same way: reschedule to a convenient date or refund the prepayment.
Is a photographer included?
After the flight — yes. Our photographer works with you for 5–10 minutes and delivers 10–20 edited shots. These are keepsake photos and they are included in the price.
A full photo shoot is a separate service: for brands, model agencies, Love Story and weddings. It can be booked without a flight.
Is a prepayment required?
Yes. We hold a specific time only after prepayment — until then the date stays open for other guests. If we reschedule the flight, the prepayment is kept or fully refunded.
Can I shoot the helicopter without flying?
Yes, it is a separate service. The helicopter stands on the pad with engines off; you can shoot outside and inside the cabin. Up to 10 people, one to two hours — the price is the same.
Airbus H125 — 30,000 KGS, Airbus H145 — 35,000 KGS. On weekdays, shoots end by 17:00. At weekends there is a 50% surcharge per aircraft.
What should I bring?
For routes with a mountain landing — warm clothes and sunglasses: at 3,500 m it is cool even in summer. A passport is required: passenger details are submitted in advance. Bring your phone and camera — filming in flight is allowed.
How safe is it?
We fly 2022 Airbus helicopters. Every pilot has more than 20 years in aviation and 8,000+ flight hours, a medical check every 6 months and simulator training. Before every flight — a briefing and safety instruction.
