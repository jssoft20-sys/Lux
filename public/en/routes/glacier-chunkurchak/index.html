Ala-Archa glacier and Chunkurchak gorge

60 min flight 30 min on the ground Landing at 3 500 m Airbus H125

What to expect

A high-altitude landing among the peaks of the Tien Shan.

A flight over the Ala-Archa gorge, mountain peaks and glaciers with a landing at the alpine camp. Half an hour on the ground — a walk, photographs and the kind of silence that exists only at this altitude.

  • Landing at 3,500 m
  • The Great Ala-Archa glacier
  • Half an hour on the ground at the alpine camp
  • Return via the Chunkurchak gorge
