Weather and confirmation

The evening before the flight, flight operations check the forecast. If the weather is flyable we confirm the time. If not, we move the flight to a convenient day or refund the prepayment.

  • 1Coordinator meets you at the gate
  • 2Briefing and safety rules — 10 minutes
  • 3Passports checked in advance
  • 4Headsets, belts, doors

0309:30

Meeting at the heliport

Your coordinator meets you at the gate and walks you to the pad. A short briefing: how to board, how to buckle up, how the headsets work, what you may film.

Start-up and take-off

Battery, fuel pump, ignition, starter. The turbine spools up within a minute, the rotor reaches 100% — and the helicopter lifts gently off the pad.

Flight over the Tien Shan

Thirty minutes to the glacier: the city, the Ala-Archa gorge, peaks and glaciers behind the panoramic windows. The pilot tells you what is below.

0810:30 – 11:00

Mountain landing — 30 minutes

The helicopter lands by the alpine camp at 3,500 m. Half an hour of silence, photographs, tea — the crew stays nearby.

  • 5–10 minutes with the photographer
  • 10–20 keepsake photos the same day
  • Flight video — on request

0911:00 – 11:40

Return and photographs

Back via the Chunkurchak gorge and a landing on the pad. After the flight, 5–10 minutes with the photographer — and 10–20 edited shots to keep.
